Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you play Berry's Strawberry Cake and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

When you play the game, we may collect information about your game session, including:

  • IP address
  • Approximate country, state or region, and city based on the IP address
  • Device type
  • Browser and user-agent information
  • Game score
  • Number of strikes
  • Game time
  • Number of attempts
  • Game session start and completion times

Why We Collect This Information

This information is used to keep track of game sessions, understand how the game is being played, troubleshoot problems, improve the game, and help identify unusual or potentially invalid game results.

Location Information

Your approximate location may be determined from your IP address. This location is not your exact physical location and may be inaccurate. For example, an IP address may be associated with a nearby city rather than the city where you are actually located.

How Information Is Stored

Game session information is sent to a Cloudflare Worker and stored in a Cloudflare D1 database.

Information Sharing

We do not use the information collected through the game for advertising purposes. Information may be processed or stored by the services used to operate the game, including Cloudflare.

Data Retention

Game session information may be retained for as long as it is needed for operating, troubleshooting, and improving the game.

Children's Privacy

Berry's Strawberry Cake is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without the appropriate parental permissions where required by law.
